About This Experience

Not every great Sognefjord day needs to be a mountain challenge. This experience is designed for guests who want to see the best of the Sognefjord region — waterfalls, fjord views, village character and local coffee — at a relaxed pace that anyone can enjoy.

The day begins with a private guided walk on the Fossestien trail along the Gaula River, passing a selection of the fourteen waterfalls that make this one of the most scenic easy trails in western Norway. Your guide chooses the section and pace to suit your group — whether that is a gentle one-hour stroll or a longer valley walk to the mountain lakes.

From the waterfall trail, the experience moves to Høyanger village itself — one of the most distinctive fjord communities in Vestland, built around one of Norway's first major hydroelectric power schemes. Your guide brings the history of the village to life against the backdrop of the fjord before a stop at a local coffee spot to round off the day.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is this experience suitable for non-hikers?

Yes — this is our most accessible experience, designed for guests who want a full Sognefjord day without a demanding hike. The waterfall walk is easy and can be adjusted to the group's pace. Suitable for older visitors, families with young children and anyone who prefers a gentle pace.

What is included in the experience?

The experience includes a private guided waterfall walk on the Fossestien trail, a guided tour of Høyanger village with fjord viewpoints, and a local coffee stop. All guide fees are included. Transport to Høyanger and personal gear are not included.

What is special about Høyanger village?

Høyanger is one of the most distinctive fjord villages in Vestland — built around one of Norway's first major hydroelectric power schemes in the early 20th century. The village architecture, the industrial heritage and the fjordside setting combine to create something genuinely unlike anything else on the Sognefjord. Your guide brings this history to life.

Can this experience be combined with a ferry trip?

Yes — Høyanger is served by the Sognefjord ferry network and a fjord crossing can be incorporated into the day. Ask about this when you enquire and we will arrange the logistics.

Is this suitable for children?

Very much so — this is our most family-friendly experience. The walk is easy and can be as short as an hour. The village and waterfall sections hold children's interest well. Your guide will tailor the day to the pace of your youngest walker.
